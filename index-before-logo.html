Meet Pisgah Payments

Local relationships. Regional reach.

We’re Adam and Courtney Kelly—business partners, life partners and the owners of Pisgah Payments. We built this company around a simple idea: merchants deserve honest guidance and a support relationship that feels personal.

Our roots are in Western North Carolina near the Pisgah National Forest, and today we serve businesses across North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia and beyond. As our footprint has grown, our approach has stayed the same: listen first, recommend thoughtfully and remain available when our merchants need us.

We work closely with restaurants, automotive businesses, retailers, service providers and community organizations. Whether you need a complete POS system, better processing options or help understanding your current costs, you’ll work directly with us.

Our merchants aren’t account numbers. They’re people we know, support and grow alongside.
